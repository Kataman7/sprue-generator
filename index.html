🏭 Why use a sprue with a protective cage?

In industrial powder-bed 3D printing processes such as Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) or Multi Jet Fusion (MJF) in Nylon PA12/PA11, service bureaus like JLCPCB and PCBWay charge manual handling fees per individual piece and impose minimum part size limits (often 15–20 mm) to prevent loss during powder cake de-caking and sieving.

By consolidating multiple parts into a closed sprue with an outer protective runner frame, the entire build is treated as a single unified part during automated quoting. This drastically reduces unit costs while preventing delicate parts from damage during transport and tumbling.

✂️ Break-Away Gate & Runner Design

Designed for fast, clean, tool-free post-processing upon arrival:

  • Breakaway Neck (Ø 0.5 to 1.5 mm): Located directly at the contact junction with the part surface, concentrating mechanical stress so tabs snap off cleanly with minimal witness marks.
  • Tab Stem Body: Robust section automatically proportioned to 70% of runner diameter, ensuring parts do not vibrate or detach prematurely during industrial de-powdering and bead-blasting.
  • Protective Outer Frame: Rigid boundary perimeter that absorbs shipping and handling impacts.
