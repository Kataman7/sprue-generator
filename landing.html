The Magic of the Engine

Automated by default for speed. Fully customizable for power users.

Standard boolean operations in traditional CAD often produce degenerate micro-faces, self-intersections, and non-manifold edges that fail factory slicers. SprueGen builds clean geometric primitives with dedicated breakaway necks.

Zero Part Penetration (embedDepth = 0.0)

The attach neck strictly docks against the outer normal boundary of your model. No cylinder enters your hollow cavities or distorts critical internal screw threads.

Tapered Breakaway Neck (1.2 mm Ø)

Conical neck transition focuses shear stress precisely at the gate surface. Break off parts by hand or with flush cutters in 1 second without sanding.

Flat-Bed Z-Resting Alignment

Parts align perfectly to Z=0 flat against the printer bed, preventing vertical build height inflation and minimizing support material usage.
